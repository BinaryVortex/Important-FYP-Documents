## 📌 About This Repository

This repository serves as a central hub for the important documents, research papers, and literature review materials collected throughout the **Final Year Project**.

The purpose of this repository is to keep all research-related resources organized and easily accessible throughout the different stages of the FYP — from **initial research and topic exploration** to **literature review, research gap identification, and project development**.

## 🔬 Research Papers

### 📚 arXiv & Springer

This folder contains research papers gathered from **arXiv and Springer** that are relevant to the selected FYP research areas.

The papers are used to:

- Understand existing research
- Identify commonly used methodologies
- Explore current technologies and approaches
- Analyze limitations of existing solutions
- Identify potential research gaps
- Support the development of the FYP research direction

### 📖 IEEE Research Papers

This folder contains relevant research papers obtained from **IEEE**.

These papers provide additional academic research and technical perspectives that support the literature review and help establish the research foundation of the project.

## 📊 Literature Review Matrix

The repository includes:

**`FYP_Literature_Review_Matrix.xlsx`**

This spreadsheet is used to systematically organize and analyze the collected research papers.

The matrix can include information such as:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| 📄 Paper | Research paper title |
| 👨‍🔬 Authors | Paper authors |
| 📅 Year | Publication year |
| 🔬 Methodology | Research methodology / approach |
| 🛠️ Technologies | Technologies or techniques used |
| 📊 Findings | Key findings |
| ⚠️ Limitations | Identified limitations |
| 🔎 Research Gap | Potential research gap |
| 💡 Relevance | Relevance to the FYP |
| 🔗 Reference | Paper/source link |
